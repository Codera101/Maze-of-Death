HOW TO PLAY ?

  1. CONTROLS

    • Arrow Keys

    • Shoot: Spacebar

  2. GAME RULES

    • Start with 3 health and 10 bullets

    • You can only see and shoot players directly in front of you

    • Each successful hit: +5 points for you, -5 points and -1 health for target

    • When health reaches 0, you're eliminated and respawn after 3 seconds

  3. TIPS
    • Watch your ammo count - running out leaves you defenseless
    • Watch your ammo count - running out leaves you defenseless
