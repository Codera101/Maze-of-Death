# Maze-of-Death

- Maze of Death is a fast, real-time multiplayer maze shooter with human players and AI bots and a spectator (viewer) mode.
- Players spawn randomly with limited health and bullets, and can move up/down/left/right.
- Players can only see and shoot others when someone is directly in front of them.
- Shooting awards +5 points to the shooter and costs the target −5 points; the target disappears and respawns in a random empty cell after 2 seconds.
- Reloading costs 5 points; if a player cannot pay to reload when out of bullets, they lose and are removed.
- Players can pause (becoming frozen and temporarily removed from the maze) and later return with their previous state.
- A player panel shows all players (colored names), ranked by points, with online/offline indicators; dead/removed players are taken off the panel.
- Viewer mode streams a live view of all players and combat for non-participating observers.
- AI-controlled agents fill games so the maze stays active and gameplay is continuous.
